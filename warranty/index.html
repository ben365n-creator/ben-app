What the Warranty Does Not Cover

Concrete is a natural material with predictable limitations. The warranty covers our workmanship — not conditions outside our control.

  • Normal surface wear over time
  • Resealing after the agreed-upon initial seal period
  • Damage from improper maintenance or use
  • Settlement caused by soil conditions outside the contractor's control
  • Hairline shrinkage cracks within normal industry tolerances
  • Damage from acts of weather, freeze-thaw cycles, or de-icing chemicals
  • Modifications made to the concrete after project completion
  • Damage caused by tree roots, heavy vehicle loading beyond design, or third-party work

Specific warranty duration and exclusion language are included in your written project proposal and reviewed before any project begins.
