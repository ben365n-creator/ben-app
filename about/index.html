The Founder

Ben Norris

865 Titan Concrete is a one-owner company. There's no franchise, no absentee investor, no call center. It's Ben — licensed in Tennessee, insured, and personally responsible for every project that goes out under this name.

The crews Ben works with are experienced tradespeople he's vetted and worked alongside. He doesn't hand off the job and disappear. He's on-site, accessible by phone, and the same person you'll talk to from the first call through the final walk-through.

That matters on a concrete project, where a bad pour or a skipped step doesn't show up as a visible crack until long after a crew has left. Concrete rewards preparation and punishes shortcuts — so the only way to stand behind the work is to be there for it.
